Privacy policy

This explains what we collect when you send us a paper, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • The student's first name, class, board, subject and type of exam
  • The question paper and answer sheet you upload, and optionally the teacher-marked copy and school score
  • The parent or guardian's name, WhatsApp number and, if given, email

We do not ask for addresses, school names or other details we don't need.

Why we use it

  • To prepare the analysis you asked for and send it to you
  • To run any session or re-test you choose to book
  • To improve the pilot service, for example by checking how accurate our analysis was

We never publish answer sheets or share them with other students or families.

Who processes it

Our team reviews every analysis before it is shared. Software and cloud services (for example, for file storage, forms and AI-assisted analysis) help us process the papers. We use them only for the purposes above.

How long we keep it

We keep papers and reports for as long as the pilot runs, so we can compare re-tests with the first paper. After that we delete them, unless you ask us to keep them.

Request deletion

You can ask us to delete your child's papers and reports at any time. Message us on WhatsApp with the student's first name and the number you used to submit, and we will confirm once it's done.

Children's information

Most students are under 18. We only accept submissions from a parent or guardian, or from someone who has their permission, and the form asks you to confirm this.
