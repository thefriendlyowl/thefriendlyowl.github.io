FAQ

Questions parents ask

Which papers can I send?

Any CBSE Class 10 Maths or Science paper: pre-board, school test, or a mock. Please send the question paper along with the answer sheet.

Photos or PDF?

A PDF is best, but clear phone photos of every page work too. Please keep pages in order and take them in good light.

How long does the report take?

Usually within 48 hours during the pilot.

Is the marking done by AI?

AI does the first pass, so it is fast and consistent, and a teacher checks every report before it is shared. Marks are our best estimate, not official board marks.

What happens to my child's answer sheet?

It is used only to prepare your report and plan. We never share it or show it publicly.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. The checkup and report are yours to keep. Practice tests and 1:1 sessions are optional.
