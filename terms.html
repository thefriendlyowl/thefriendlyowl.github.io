Pilot terms

The Friendly Owl is running a pilot for CBSE Class 10 Mathematics and Science. By submitting a paper you agree to these terms.

The free paper analysis

  • During the pilot, the first paper analysis is free.
  • It is an educational review to help you decide what to work on next. Any marks shown are our estimate, not an official board or school result.
  • We may ask for a clearer photo or scan if pages are hard to read.

Focused Improvement Sessions

  • Pilot price: ₹199 for one 45-minute 1:1 online session, plus one targeted practice/re-test checked by our team.
  • Booking is optional and separate from the free analysis.
  • Rescheduling and refunds: please message us at least 12 hours before the session.

No guarantees

We aim to help students improve, but we cannot guarantee any particular score or result.

Consent

By submitting, you confirm that you are the student's parent or guardian, or have their permission to submit this work for analysis.

Changes

As this is a pilot, we may update the service or these terms. We will tell families who are actively using the service about any important change.
