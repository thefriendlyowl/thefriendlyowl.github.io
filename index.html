Our approach

Not another full tuition course

A student who loses marks in one or two specific areas may not need another months-long programme. We start with what the student actually wrote, then decide whether they need practice, explanation, a focused session, or help correcting a recurring mistake.

AI-assisted analysis · Human-reviewed

Technology helps us analyse. People remain responsible.

Software helps us organise and analyse answer sheets efficiently. Every pilot report is reviewed by a member of our team before it reaches a student or parent. Unclear handwriting, diagrams, alternative methods and marking decisions are checked manually.

Your child's work stays private

Answer sheets are used only to provide the analysis you asked for and to improve the pilot. They are never published or shared with other students. Software and cloud services may help process them, and our team reviews every analysis before it is shared with you.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the paper analysis really free?

Yes. During the pilot, the first paper analysis is free.

Which subjects are currently supported?

CBSE Class 10 Mathematics and Science.

Do I have to book a paid session?

No. The paper analysis is independent.

How much is a focused session?

The pilot price is ₹199. It includes one 45-minute focused session and one targeted practice/re-test that we check afterwards.

Who conducts the sessions?

Members of our team: engineers and software professionals with experience teaching school students. The mentor is chosen by subject and topic.

Is this normal tuition?

No. We start from the student's actual test performance and focus only on the areas that need help.

Is the answer sheet graded only by AI?

No. Software assists with the analysis, and our team reviews every pilot report.

What if the handwriting is difficult to read?

We review the page manually, or ask you for a clearer photo or scan.

What happens to uploaded papers?

They are handled under our privacy policy and used only for the purposes described there. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
