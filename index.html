FAQ

Common questions

Is the paper analysis really free?

Yes. During the pilot, the first CBSE Class 10 Maths or Science paper analysis for a student is free.

What happens after the free analysis?

Subsequent paper reviews are paid and are priced based on the total marks being reviewed. Smaller tests cost less than full exam papers.

How much does a paid paper review cost?

Pilot pricing starts at ₹49 for shorter tests. The review price depends on the total marks covered by the paper or selected questions, and we confirm the price before starting.

Can I submit only a few questions instead of a full paper?

Yes. You can submit a chapter test, selected questions or part of an exam. Pricing is based on the total marks being reviewed.

Is this an AI grading service?

No. Technology helps us analyse papers, but every pilot report is human-reviewed. The goal is not simply to assign marks. It is to identify where marks are being lost and what the student should work on next.

Do you guarantee the same marks as a CBSE examiner?

No. School and board marking can involve examiner judgment. We use CBSE-style marking principles and focus primarily on identifying learning and answer-writing gaps.

Do I need to join tuition afterwards?

No. The report may recommend practice, revision or a focused teaching session, depending on the gap.

Who conducts the focused sessions?

Our focused sessions are conducted by members of The Friendly Owl mentoring team, who have several years of experience teaching Mathematics and Science.

Which subjects are supported?

The current pilot supports CBSE Class 10 Mathematics and Science.

Can I upload a pre-board paper?

Yes. Pre-board, school test and mock papers are ideal.

What if handwriting is unclear?

We manually review uncertain answers. If a page cannot be read reliably, we may request a clearer image.
