FAQ

About the pilot

Is the first paper analysis really free?

Yes. During the pilot, the first CBSE Class 10 Maths or Science paper analysis for a student is free.

What happens after the free analysis?

Future Performance Reviews are paid and are priced based on the total marks being reviewed. Smaller tests and selected questions cost less than full exam papers.

How much does a Performance Review cost after the free analysis?

Pilot pricing is based on the total marks being reviewed:

  • Up to 20 marks₹49
  • 21–40 marks₹79
  • 41–60 marks₹119
  • 61–80 marks₹149

We confirm the price before starting the review. The same pricing applies to Maths and Science. If the marks aren’t clear on the paper, we confirm the price after checking your submission.

Can I submit only part of a paper?

Yes. You can submit selected questions, a chapter test or part of an exam. Pricing for future Performance Reviews is based on the total marks being reviewed.

Do I have to book a Focused Improvement Session?

No. The paper analysis is independent.

How much is a focused session?

The pilot price is ₹199. It includes one 45-minute focused session and one targeted practice/re-test that we check afterwards.

Who conducts the focused sessions?

Our focused sessions are conducted by members of The Friendly Owl mentoring team, who have several years of experience teaching Mathematics and Science.

Is this normal tuition?

No. We start from the student's actual test performance and focus only on the areas that need help.

What if the handwriting is difficult to read?

We review the page manually, or ask you for a clearer photo or scan.

What happens to uploaded papers?

They are handled under our privacy policy. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
