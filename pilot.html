FAQ

Common questions

Who can join this pilot?

CBSE Class 10 students, for Maths (Standard or Basic) or Science.

What exactly should I send?

The question paper and your child's answer sheet from the same test. We need both to review the answers properly.

Photos or PDF?

A PDF is best, but clear phone photos of every page are fine. Good light, pages in order.

How long does it take?

Usually within two days.

Who sees my child's papers?

Only the three of us. They are used only to prepare your report, never shared, and we delete them whenever you ask.

Why is it free?

This is our first batch. We want to learn whether our reports are genuinely useful, and your feedback is what we need most right now.
